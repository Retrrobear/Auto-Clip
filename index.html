✦ AutoClip
Free setup · Personal use

Turn long videos into short clips.

AutoClip uses your own Google Drive, Google account and YouTube channel. Video processing runs in your own Google Colab session — this website does not store your Google password or process videos on a paid server.

Ready to create clips?

Open the AutoClip Colab notebook, connect your accounts there, then run the workflow. If the notebook link has not been configured by the site owner yet, use the setup instructions below.

↗ Start in Google Colab Open Google Drive

The site owner needs to add the shared Colab notebook link before this button can launch the workflow.

First-time setup

STEP 01

Open the notebook

Tap “Start in Google Colab”. Sign in with your own Google account.

STEP 02

Connect Drive

Run the notebook's Drive mount cell and approve Google's permission screen.

STEP 03

Connect YouTube

Follow the notebook's YouTube authorization steps. Never enter your Google password into this website.

STEP 04

Choose your video

Put the source video in the folder and path specified in the notebook, then run AutoClip.

When you come back later

The website itself does not keep you signed in. Colab sessions can expire or disconnect. If that happens, reopen the notebook, reconnect Drive and YouTube if requested, and run the workflow again. Keep your original video until you have checked the YouTube uploads.

Google Colab Free is not guaranteed to run continuously. It may stop a session due to usage limits or inactivity. The website can stay online, but processing only happens while your Colab runtime is active.
